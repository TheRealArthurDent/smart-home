Influx measurements and grafana dashboards

Measurement naming

<DeviceType>.<Room>.<Function>.<Attribute>

<DeviceType>: Sensor, Button, Switch, Dimmer
<Room>: Livingroom, Office, Bedroom, Bathroom, ...
<Function>: Whatever - may be separated by colons :
            e.g. Light:Desk
<Attribute>: measured attribute (on, battery, reachable, temperature)




Sensor.Office.Temperature.reachable
